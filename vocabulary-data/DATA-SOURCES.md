## Uzbek word equivalents

Short lexical equivalents were adapted and checked against the public English-Uzbek reference at https://github.com/qobulovasror/eng-uzb-dictionary (words/uzb-eng.json). Extended definitions, dictionary examples, and the complete reference dictionary are not included in this archive. Missing equivalents and reviewed corrections were written for this deliverable. The reference repository does not specify a license, so no blanket open-source license is asserted for the complete combined dataset. The Openjam license below applies to the Openjam-derived material only.

These are partially reviewed vocabulary drafts. Automatic field validation does not certify linguistic correctness, sense alignment, or CEFR suitability of every example.
